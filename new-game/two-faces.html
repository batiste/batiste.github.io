Two Faces

Aeropolis, back to the core: one city on the edge of revolution, two characters per player, 2–4 players.

Playtest

You play both sides: a Citizen at the top of the Spire, an Outcast at its base. Each turn, take a card and tuck it above or below one of your characters: that column activates, every card in it. When your two characters meet, the game ends. You score the height of your lower one.

Setup

  1. Each player: a Citizen card and an Outcast card side by side; Citizen pawn on Spire , Outcast pawn on 1; .
  2. Shuffle the Citizen and Outcast decks. Reveal cards from each: the row.
  3. Shuffle the contraband deck and the Uprising deck. Reveal the first Uprising.

Round

  1. Starting with the first player, each player takes 1 turn, twice around the table.
  2. Resolve the Uprising (see Uprising).
  3. The first player passes left. Reveal the next Uprising.

Turn

Take one:

  • Recruit: take 1 card from the row. Pay its cost: Sink your Citizen that many. Tuck it above or below its character, then activate that side. Refill the row from the same deck.
  • Scheme: look at the top card of each deck. Place 1 face down in front of you, the other at the bottom of its deck.

Empty deck: shuffle its discards into it.

Sides

Each character has 2 sides: tucked cards above it, tucked cards below it. A card tucked above shows only its top strip; below, only its bottom strip. A side holds strips: tucking another discards its oldest.

Activate: resolve the side's strips from newest to oldest, then the character's own strip. "→" effects are optional.

Contraband

GoodUse

Street run: draw contraband cards one at a time. Each shows goods and heat; a Patrol shows only heat. Stop whenever you like and take the goods drawn. If your total heat goes over your heat limit, you are caught: you gain nothing. Either way, shuffle the drawn cards back.

Uprising

  1. Each player secretly commits any number of their Arms, then all reveal them together with their Schemes.
  2. Strength = your +N Schemes + Arms committed, doubled for each ×2 Scheme. Strongest gets the first reward, next gets the second. Strength 0 gets nothing. Tie: lower Outcast wins, then turn order.
  3. Discard all Schemes and committed Arms.

Spire

  • Your Outcast never goes above your Citizen. You cannot pay a cost that would put your Citizen below your Outcast.
  • When they stand on the same height, they meet.

End

  • The game ends after the Uprising of the round in which any player's characters meet, or after the last Uprising.
  • Score: the height of your lower pawn. Tie: higher Citizen.

Playtest Questions

  • Game length: do characters meet before the last Uprising? Tune costs, Deal and rewards.
  • Engine: is a cap of strips per side too tight, too loose?
  • Cost: is sinking the Citizen a real price, since only the lower pawn scores?
  • Street run: is heat limit 5 with Patrols fun, or too safe / too swingy?
  • Scheme: is giving up a Recruit worth it? Is ×2 a fun bluff or a trap?
  • Loud Citizen (later): each Citizen activation adds heat to your next run?
  • Characters: all identical for now. Unique base strips per character later?
