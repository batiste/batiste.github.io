Aeropolis — Prototype Cards

Two characters and the seven-card starter deck. A deck card reads two ways: played into a character line, or kept in hand through the discard phase. The face-down Intrigue mode is parked for now to keep the core loop testable — its text is still in the file behind the SHOW_INTRIGUE flag. All numbers are first-pass placeholders. Print at true 57 × 89 mm with ⌘P.

Characters One of each per player, in play from setup. They anchor the left end of their line.

    Starter Deck — 7 cards Identical for every player. Playing card in a location activate the location benefit and you take the cube. Each card in your deck can socket a cube. If there are no cube in a location, you can still go but the cube effect do not apply.

      Market — Coin-cost samples Bought from a market spot for coin before joining your hand — everything after that follows the same slotted-card template as the starter deck, still recruiting and socketing a cube to play. Priced on a rough 1 coin ≈ 1 Rise-step curve, so the four samples trace a cheap-to-priced power curve.
