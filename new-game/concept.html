Aeropolis

Cards → Board →

From the decadent high council to the ruthless underworld, no one in Aeropolis is clean. Here, power sprouts from greed and blackmail, driving the city toward revolution. Play both sides from the shadows, engineer a silent coup, and eliminate your rivals to seize absolute control.

The Dynamic

Each player begins with two distinct characters (2 cards): a Citizen and an Outcast. These characters form the left ends of their tableau, with cards played into either character's line. There are 5 Citizens and 5 Outcasts, creating 25 possible starting combinations.

Central Track

A central vertical track runs along the spire of the city board. Each player places their Citizen token at the top and their Outcast token at the bottom of this track. The game ends when they meet (or after a number of turns).

The higher up the track the two characters meet, the more Victory Points (VPs) the player scores. VPs might be scored by other means.

Track Regress as a Currency

Players can spend progress by moving a character down the track to generate resources (e.g. to buy more cards or activate effects). Descending is necessary, but lowers your endgame VP yield and might reduce access to certain actions.

Deckbuilding

Tight construction: starting decks are small and cycle rapidly (aiming for 7–8 reshuffles per game).

Dual card value — played vs. unplayed/discarded

You do not need to play your entire hand every turn.

Played cards

Executed in a character line, socketed with cubes, or played face down as Intrigue (traps/bribes).

Discarded / unplayed cards

Keeping a card unplayed at the end of the turn might grant a resource or move you up a track.

Going to Locations

Board reservoirs (0–2 cubes): board locations (The Rust Bar, The High Forum, The Market, etc.) each hold 0–2 Agent cubes and automatically replenish each round. A location can only be used while it has an available cube; once its cubes are gone, it is unavailable until the next replenishment.

Recruiting an Agent: to use a location, recruit one of its available cubes and socket it onto the card you played in your line.

Turn Sequence

  1. Draw phase

    Everybody draws a hand (4 cards) from their deck of 7 (3 cards left).

  2. Action phase — going around the table for each player

    If you wish, end your turn and go to the discard phase. Otherwise: play 1 card to the Citizen or Outcast line, "recruit" 1 cube (mandatory) from the board to socket into the played card, then activate the effect of the card and the location in any order.

  3. Discard phase

    Discard your remaining hand to trigger a bonus (up the coin track?).

  4. Resolution & reset

    Replenish board location cubes (typically 1). Random effects or the number of players might vary the amount.
