Aeropolis

Draft rules for the current playtest version.

Concept Cards Board

Each player manages a Citizen and an Outcast. Play cards into their two lines, slot cubes from the city, and decide when to spend status for resources. This is an early draft: values, card effects, scoring beyond the Spire, and some train details are still being tested.

Setup

  1. Give each player one Citizen character and one Outcast character.
  2. Place the Citizen at the top of the Spire and the Outcast at the bottom.
  3. Give each player the current example starter deck of 7 cards.
  4. Give each player 2 coins.
  5. Fill each board location with up to 2 cubes.
  6. Place the train at its starting stop and make its seats available.

The character pool, player count, and exact starting setup are still playtest parameters.

Round Structure

  1. Draw: each player draws 4 cards from their deck.
  2. Action: in turn order, each player takes exactly 1 action. After that action, play passes to the next player.
  3. Discard: a player may pass instead of taking an action. That player immediately discards their remaining hand and resolves its Keep effects. They do not take further actions this round.
  4. Replenish: after every player has passed, add 1 cube to each location, up to 2, and advance the train by 1 stop. Players empty their lines.

Taking An Action

The normal card action is to play 1 card into a line. Choose the line, pay that line's play cost, and Slot 1 from an eligible location. Resolve the location reward and the card effect in either order. Every played card has a cube socket, and the slotted cube starts there.

A card marked Any may be played into either line. A Citizen card normally belongs in the Citizen line, and an Outcast card normally belongs in the Outcast line. Train access overrides this restriction for a passenger who disembarks at the current stop.

Card effects may move a slotted cube from its current socket to the character anchoring that line or to another empty card socket. A card effect may also change a character's position, draw cards, gain coins, or modify another game element.

Boarding the train is an action and uses an available seat. Disembarking is a card-play action: play the card at the train's current stop, take the cube from the train instead of from the location, and resolve the stop's reward. The location does not need to contain a cube. A passenger may therefore disembark at an empty location.

Locations And Cubes

Each location holds 0–2 cubes. A location is available only while it has at least 1 cube. Citizen locations are eligible when playing into the Citizen line. Outcast locations are eligible when playing into the Outcast line. Shared locations, including the Noodle Shop, are eligible for either line.

If no eligible location has a cube, you usually cannot play the card. When you Slot 1, take the cube first and resolve that location's reward. A passenger who disembarks uses the train's cube instead, so the stop may be empty. The card's socket effect then resolves according to the card text.

The Noodle Shop is the shared location where cards may be bought with coins before they enter a player's hand. Its cards are provisional examples and use the same play process as starter cards.

The Train

The train has a limited number of seats. Boarding costs 1 action. Disembarking costs the passenger's card-play action: play at the train's current stop, take the cube from the train instead of the location, and resolve the stop's reward. A passenger who disembarks may use that stop even when its normal line restriction does not match their card, such as playing an Outcast card into the Citizen line.

The train advances 1 stop during Replenish. When it completes a full circuit, each passenger gains 1 coin. Additional train interactions and bonuses remain open for playtesting.

The Revolution

When a player's Citizen and Outcast meet on the Spire, the Revolution begins immediately for everyone. The player who triggered it gains 2 coins. All players flip one of their characters to its Revolution side; the Revolution side changes the character's power for the rest of the game.

The game continues with the same one-action turn structure. Normal face-up card play is unchanged. The new Revolution option is that a player may play a normal card face down into either line as an Intrigue. Slot a cube from an eligible location and place it on top of the face-down card. The card and cube stay committed to that line until the Intrigue is resolved.

At the end of each round, the train's current stop becomes the Revolution site. Reveal and resolve every eligible Intrigue there, one at a time, in descending conflict value. Resolve the Intrigue text even if that card does not win. The player with the highest conflict value, including Leader bonuses, wins the site. Discard the resolved Intrigue cards and return their cubes to the supply.

The current site determines which Intrigue cards may contest it. Citizen locations accept Citizen-line Intrigue, Outcast locations accept Outcast-line Intrigue, and shared locations accept either line. A passenger who disembarks at the site may use either line.

The winner receives the current site's Revolution reward. The location's normal reward is still resolved through normal face-up play; the Revolution reward is received only by the player who wins the Intrigue conflict.

Site Winner's reward
High Forum Rise your Citizen 2 and gain 3 coins.
Counting House Gain 5 coins.
Rust Bar Rise your Outcast 2 and force one opponent to discard 1 coin.
Scrapyard Return one discarded card to your hand and gain 2 coins.
Noodle Shop Take one available Noodle Shop card for free.
Cable Lift Rise your Outcast 2 and draw 2 cards.

The Revolution is a second phase, not the end of the game. The exact Leader powers, Intrigue values, and Revolution rewards are playtest values.

Glossary

Slot

Take 1 cube from an eligible location, resolve its reward, and place the cube in the played card's socket. Slotting is part of playing a card, not a separate action.

Recruit

Fixer's card effect. Move the cube in Fixer's socket onto the character anchoring that line. Recruit does not take a new cube from a location.

Rise

Move the named character 1 space upward on the Spire, toward the top.

Sink

Move the named character 1 space downward on the Spire, toward the bottom.

Pass

Take no action, discard your remaining hand, and wait for the next round.

End Of The Game

The game ends after 4 Revolution sites have been resolved. Players score Victory Points for their final Spire meeting position, plus any other scoring effects.

Do not treat the current card values or scoring curve as final.
