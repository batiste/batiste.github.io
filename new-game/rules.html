Aeropolis

Draft rules for the current playtest version.

Concept Cards Board

Each player manages a Citizen and an Outcast. Play cards into their two lines, slot cubes from the city, and decide when to spend status for resources. This is an early draft: everything is subject to chance.

Setup

  1. Each player get one Citizen character and one Outcast character cards (draft).
  2. Each player places their Citizen token at the top of the Spire and their Outcast token at the bottom.
  3. Give each player the current example starter deck of 7 cards.
  4. Give each player 2 coins.
  5. Create a deck of 6 random Conflict cards.
  6. Fill each board location with up to 2 cubes (mechanism TBD).
  7. Place the train at its starting stop and make its seats available.

The character pool, player count, and exact starting setup are still playtest parameters.

Round Structure

  1. Draw: each player draws 4 cards from their deck.
  2. Action: in turn order, each player takes one turn — Play, Discard, or Intrigue (see Taking An Action) — until every player has discarded and has no committed card left to resolve.
  3. Replenish: add cubes equal to 2 × the number of players, plus this round's Conflict card's Refill Bonus (see Conflict). Place them 1 at a time around the locations in board order, looping back and skipping any already at 2, until every cube is placed. Advance the train by 1 stop. Empty every line: discard every card in it, face-up or face-down, and return their cubes to the supply.

Taking An Action

Each turn is one of three actions. Each player must take an action, until they have nothing to do.

Play

Play 1 card into a line. Pay that line's play cost, then Recruit 1 cube from an eligible location. Recruiting resolves that location's reward; depending on whether you played the card face up or face down, a second effect also resolves — the player chooses which of the two effects to resolve first:

  • Face up: Slot the recruit in the card's normal socket and resolve the socket's effect.
  • Face down (Intrigue): Sink that line's character 1 as a cost, and place the cube on the top of the card (see Conflict).

Discard

If you hold any cards, you may spend your turn discarding your remaining hand and resolving each card's Discard effect.

Intrigue

When you have no cards in hand, if you have committed face-down cards, spend 1 turn resolving one of them. Choose the rightmost face-down card in your line, flip it face up, and choose where to socket the cube: the card's normal socket, or the intrigue socket.

Locations and Access

Each location holds 0–2 cubes. A location is available only while it has at least 1 cube. Citizen locations are eligible when playing into the Citizen line. Outcast locations are eligible when playing into the Outcast line. Shared locations, including the Noodle Shop, are eligible for either line.

If no eligible location has a cube, you usually cannot play the card. Recruiting takes the cube from the location and resolves that location's reward; slotting places the recruit in the card's normal socket and resolves the socket's effect — the player chooses which of the two to resolve first. A passenger who disembarks uses the train's cube instead, so the stop may be empty; the socket's effect still resolves the same way.

A card marked Broker may be played into either line. A Citizen card normally belongs in the Citizen line, and an Outcast card normally belongs in the Outcast line. Train access overrides this restriction for a passenger who disembarks at the current stop.

The Noodle Shop is the shared location where cards may be bought with coins before they enter a player's discard pile.

The Train

The train has a limited number of seats.

Boarding

To board the train, it must be at a location with a station: play a card in the appropriate line, take your colored cube from your reserve, and place it in the train's socket. If you board you do not pick a cube from that location.

Disembarking

To disembark the train, it must be at a location with a station: play a card in the appropriate line, take the cube from the train's socket and place it in the card's socket, then resolve that location's reward. If you disembark you do not pick a cube from that location. A passenger who disembarks may use that stop even when its normal line restriction does not match their card, such as playing an Outcast card into the Citizen line.

The train advances 1 stop during Replenish. When it completes a full circuit, each passenger gains 1 coin. Additional train interactions and bonuses remain open for playtesting.

Conflict

Each new round a new conflict is revealed and placed on the board. It sets that round's Reward, Condition, and Refill Bonus (see Round Structure — Replenish).

A player typically commits to the conflict with a face-down (see Taking An Action).

Once everybody has passed, it is time to resolve the conflict. A player's total for the conflict is the sum of the Intrigue values of their cards. The highest total wins and claims the reward.

The Revolution

The Revolution begins immediately when one of those 2 conditions is met:

  • A player's Citizen and Outcast meet on the Spire: this players raises his Citizen and Outcast by 2.
  • The Conflict deck is exhausted.

Each player chooses one character as their Leader and flips that character to its Revolution side; this changes the character's power for the rest of the game. Replace the Conflict pile with a pile of 3 random Revolution Conflict cards (playtest: replace the current conflict).

The Revolution is a second phase, not the end of the game.

Glossary

Recruit

Take 1 grey cube (a recruit) from an eligible location and place it in your card's socket. You then can take the location's effect, and card's socket effect, in either order.

Plant

Take a cube from the supply, and place it in any empty slot in this line: another card's socket, or the character anchoring it.

Rise

Move the named character 1 space upward on the Spire, toward the top.

Sink

Move the named character 1 space downward on the Spire, toward the bottom.

End Of The Game

The game ends after 3 Revolution rounds have been resolved. Players score Victory Points from their lowest position on the Spire, plus any other scoring effects.

Do not treat the current card values or scoring curve as final.
