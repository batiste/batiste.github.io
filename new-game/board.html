A location is unavailable once its grey cubes are gone. Citizen locations for the Citizen line, Outcast locations for the Outcast line, and shared locations for either line. The played card may socket the slotted cube to trigger its card effect.

The Line Boarding costs 1 action. Disembarking is card play: take the cube from the train, so the current stop may be empty. The train advances 1 stop during Replenish.
